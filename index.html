01 / THE OPPORTUNITYA NATIONAL PROBLEM.
A CONNECTED RESPONSE.

Housing has many moving parts. They rarely move together.

A home search can lead to questions about legitimacy, total move-in cost, rent planning, property management, maintenance and renewal. Each moment may involve a different company, professional or institution.

06 / TRUST IS THE STRATEGY

Partnerships worth building. Standards worth keeping.

We want commercial relationships that make housing clearer and more humane. Not shortcuts around users' choices or professional responsibilities.

Read FCCPC digital lending framework ↗
01

Consent is non-negotiable

Share only the personal information required for an agreed purpose, with an appropriate lawful basis and clear user notice.

02

Verified authority to market

Never describe an unauthorised listing as approved. Owners, agents and FINDA should agree their roles.

03

Finance stays with finance

Qualified financial partners retain responsibility for credit decisions, loan operations and regulatory obligations.

04

Measured, not manufactured

No fictional testimonials, partner logos, disbursal totals or claims of guaranteed transactions.
