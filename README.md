# FINDA Partnerships — public partnership experience

**Intended branded domain:** `https://partnerships.findahq.com/`

This is an independent, responsive static HTML/CSS/JavaScript landing website for business-development enquiries, not a FINDA production product update. It proposes five partner tracks: (1) property owners and developers; (2) estate agents and brokers; (3) licensed financial institutions; (4) employers; (5) maintenance/move-in/service providers.

## What is deliberately not claimed
- No partners have been signed, no business results/volume guaranteed, and no lender approval implied.
- Move-In Pay Later is *proposed*, not live. An appropriately authorised lender would own credit decisions, KYC, finance agreements, disbursement and collections, and any commercial/referral agreement must pass relevant regulatory and legal requirements (including FCCPC DEON 2025 considerations).
- Listings require agent/property-owner authority; no employee or tenant personal data may be shared improperly.
- RentPlan is not a deposit or custodial savings account; Rent Track is not proof of payment.
- The pilot stages are an illustrative proposal, not a contractual SLA or fixed launch date.

## Files
- `index.html` semantic site, five-way accessible partner selector, clear links, factual citations.
- `styles.css` cinematic green responsive layout, reduced-motion preferences, print styling.
- `script.js` partner selector, keyboard support, navigation, sourced figure animation, and contact brief builder. **No server or third-party script receives contact information.** The builder opens the user's email or WhatsApp app, with explicit sending required.
- `assets/finda-logo-original.jpg` and `assets/founder-original.jpg` are **byte-for-byte copies of the user's supplied original images**, not AI generated or altered. The visual artwork is original CSS, not generated imagery.
- `netlify.toml` static publish directory `.` without build command.
